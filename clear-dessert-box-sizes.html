Before you order +

Catalogue sizes are selection references. Confirm usable internal dimensions and the closed-lid height with a sample of your finished dessert, especially for a large tiramisu container with lid or a square tiramisu container.

Capacity does not indicate serving weight. Confirm whether the carton quantity counts box bodies, lids or complete sets. Standard boxes start at one carton; confirm the carton packing and custom branding minimum for your model.

Frequently asked questions +
How do I choose the internal dimensions of a container for a large tiramisu?

Measure the finished dessert, including toppings and any base board. Ask for the usable internal length, width and closed-lid height of the exact box and lid combination. Catalogue dimensions and capacity alone do not confirm the fit. Check a sample with your finished dessert before ordering in bulk.

Can I request samples before bulk order?

Yes. Samples are available. Sample time depends on item and customization requirements.

Can you provide custom logo stickers or paper sleeves?

Yes. We can provide custom logo stickers, paper sleeves, paper bags, ribbons and acrylic cocoa stencils for dessert brands.

What information should I send for quotation?

Please send item number, size, quantity, destination country and custom branding requirements.

Are these boxes suitable for tiramisu and bakery takeaway?

Yes. These clear PS / PET dessert boxes are suitable for tiramisu, mousse cake, cheesecake, bakery takeaway and catering desserts.

Can you help recommend a packaging kit?

Yes. We can recommend clear boxes, stickers, sleeves, paper bags, ribbons and cocoa stencils according to your application scene.

Need a size recommendation?

Send the model, quantity and destination. We can confirm fit, packing and price.
