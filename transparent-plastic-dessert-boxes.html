Buying information

Choosing a clear dessert box

Match the finished dessert, topping and base to the usable space under the lid. The range includes rectangular containers and trays, square portion boxes and round dessert containers. Request a sample of the exact model before a bulk order when fit matters.

Share the SKU, intended dessert size, order quantity and destination. Confirm the material, matching lid, current carton packing and documents relevant to your market when requesting a factory quotation. Logo stickers, paper sleeves and other branding accessories can be discussed separately.

Frequently asked questions
How should I check the fit between a tiramisu container and its lid before a bulk order?

Record the exact box and matching lid references. Fit a sample with your finished dessert and base board, check clearance above the toppings, and check closure around the full rim. Evaluate handling with the intended filled weight, storage conditions and outer packaging. Record the sample, conditions and agreed acceptance criteria before approving the order. A closed lid alone is not evidence of leak resistance or transport performance.

What is the difference between a transparent PS and PET dessert box?

Material selection should be based on the intended dessert, format, clarity requirement, lid style, packing method and destination-market needs. The relevant product and supporting documents should be confirmed for the selected model.

Can I request a box with a custom logo?

Compatible paper sleeves, logo stickers, ribbons and related packaging accessories can be discussed. Artwork, quantity and production feasibility are confirmed before quotation.

Are factory prices listed publicly?

No. Final pricing depends on the selected model, quantity, material, packaging scope and destination. Send the SKU or target dessert size for an accurate quotation.
