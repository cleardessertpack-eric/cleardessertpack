Wholesale FAQ

Answers before you request a tiramisu box quote.

What information should I send for a wholesale tiramisu packaging quotation?

Send your preferred model or finished dessert dimensions, quantity per size, matching-lid requirement, destination country and delivery location, intended storage and transport conditions, branding needs and required delivery date. State whether you need samples and any product documents. If a detail is undecided, mark it for review so the quotation can identify assumptions and exclusions.

Use the wholesale enquiry brief above to prepare your request.

Which size should I choose for a large tiramisu?

Start with finished dessert dimensions and lid clearance. Compare HK-28188 (4000 ml) and HK-034 (4500 ml) for larger formats, then confirm internal usable space with an actual sample.

How can I compare the total cost of wholesale tiramisu containers with lids?

Request the same complete set and quantity from each supplier. Compare body and lid costs, branding, tooling where applicable, carton packing, freight and the stated delivery terms. Identify any destination charges excluded from the offer before comparing totals.

Are matching lids included in the quotation and carton quantity?

Ask for a written component breakdown for the selected SKU. Catalogue carton quantities alone do not establish whether the quotation covers bodies, lids or complete sets. Confirm the matching lid reference, quantity of each component and packing unit before ordering.

Can I test samples before placing a bulk order?

Sample arrangements can be discussed for the selected models. Confirm availability, sample charges, freight and dispatch timing before shipment. Use the samples to check actual dessert fit, lid closure, handling and presentation before approving the bulk specification.

Where can I find durable and affordable tiramisu containers with lids?

For bulk purchasing, shortlist suppliers that provide model specifications, matching lids, representative samples and a clear delivered-cost breakdown. Clear Dessert Pack provides PS and PET catalogue options for comparison. Confirm performance through your agreed sample checks and compare the total pack cost rather than assuming the lowest unit price is the best fit.

How are MOQ, mixed-model orders and lead times confirmed?

The minimum order for standard boxes is one carton. Confirm the selected model and the number of bodies, lids or complete sets per carton. Mixed-model orders, custom sizes, branding, samples and lead times require separate confirmation in your quotation.

Can I mix boxes with custom branding accessories?

Compatible sleeves, stickers, bags and ribbons can be reviewed for the chosen box. Confirm accessory dimensions, artwork, quantity and packing before quotation. See our custom branding options.

Are eco-friendly bulk options available?

Our current comparison lists PS and PET formats. Requirements for recycled content, compostability or an environmental declaration need a separate product and documentation review. See our environmental purchasing guide before specifying a claim.

Are wholesale prices published on this page?

Final prices depend on the model, quantities, components, packing and delivery scope. Send the SKU or target size and destination for a project quotation.
