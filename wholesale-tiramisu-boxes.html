Buyer checklist

What procurement teams should confirm.

01

Dessert fit

Confirm the finished dessert length, width, height, decoration clearance and whether it will be packed cold, transported or displayed in retail.

02

Commercial scope

Share estimated order quantity, destination market and whether you need samples, stock models or a custom packaging combination.

03

Branding needs

Identify whether a sleeve, sticker, ribbon, bag or insert is needed, and provide artwork files when available for feasibility review.
