Buying information

Clear tiramisu boxes for bulk orders

Choose a single-serve box, family-size container or large sharing tray. Our range includes clear PS and PET formats. Compare the finished dessert with a sample to check the usable space and matching lid before ordering. Send the SKU, quantity and destination for a wholesale quotation.

For individual desserts, compare the 240 ml SSM-002, 450 ml SSM-004 and 500 ml SSM-016 models. The stated capacity helps narrow the choice, but your layers, topping and lid clearance decide the finished fit.

For larger portions, the 1250 ml SM-040, 1300 ml E1300 and 1800 ml HK-21157 offer rectangular formats. The 4000 ml HK-28188 and 4500 ml HK-034 suit larger presentations. Frequently asked questions
What is the minimum order?

Standard boxes start from one carton. The pieces per carton depend on the model; send the SKU to confirm the current packing and quotation.

Do the tiramisu containers come with lids?

Matching lids are available for the listed formats. Confirm the lid reference and whether the quotation covers box bodies, lids or complete sets for your chosen SKU.

Are the clear tiramisu boxes acrylic?

Some buyers call these clear boxes acrylic tiramisu boxes. The listed clear models are specified as PS or PET. Check the material for each SKU before ordering.

Which box for tiramisu should I choose?

Start with your finished dessert, including its top layer and decoration. Compare the shape and dimensions on each product card, then check how much room the matching lid leaves above the filling. A square cup, round cup and rectangular tray can hold similar stated volumes but display different portions. Request a sample of the exact SKU when fit matters. Check that it works with your shelf, display and transport setup before placing a carton order.

What should I include in a tiramisu box packaging enquiry?

Send the SKU or the size you need, your expected quantity and the delivery destination. Tell us whether you need box bodies, matching lids or complete sets. If you are comparing two sizes, include the finished dessert dimensions and your preferred shape. We can then confirm the model, pieces per carton and the scope of the quotation. A photo or drawing of your portion can also make the size discussion clearer.

How do I compare carton quantities and materials?

The pieces per carton vary by SKU. For example, SSM-002 lists 294 pieces per carton, while HK-034 lists 12. Use the number shown on each card to estimate how many cartons you need; confirm the current packing when requesting a quote. The listed clear models use PS or PET, as specified on their cards. Choose by the model's dimensions, shape and material requirements, then check the finished dessert with a sample before ordering.

How do I calculate cartons for my planned order?

First estimate the number of finished portions you expect to pack. Choose a model that fits one portion, then divide the number of required pieces by that model's listed pieces per carton. Round up to whole cartons for an initial estimate. For example, the 450 ml SSM-004 lists 200 pieces per carton. A plan for 600 pieces would therefore start at three cartons of that model. The 500 ml SSM-016 lists 147 pieces per carton; reaching at least 600 pieces would take five cartons. These examples help you plan quantities, but the models have different shapes and dimensions. Do not substitute one for the other without checking the finished dessert. Confirm current carton packing, the matching lid quantity and the final quotation for your selected SKU. If you need more than one model, give the expected quantity for each model separately so the order is clear.

What should I check with a sample before ordering?

Use your actual recipe and portion size for the trial. Fill the sample to the intended level, add the final topping, and check the space under the matching lid. Confirm the footprint in your display, storage shelf and delivery packaging. Check how the closed box looks from the side and whether the decoration touches the lid. If you apply a paper sleeve or label, test it on the filled sample as well. Note the exact SKU and lid reference after the trial; similar capacities do not always mean the same shape or usable height. Ask for the material and product documents relevant to your market when requesting the quotation. For a fuller checklist, read our sample testing guide. Use the size chart to compare other models before you settle on a carton quantity.
