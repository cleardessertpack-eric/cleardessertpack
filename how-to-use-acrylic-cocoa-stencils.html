Custom cocoa stencils for wedding & event tiramisu

By Lucy

Cocoa stencilsPublished July 21, 2026

Test the lettering and lid clearance on the finished dessert

A custom cocoa stencil can help identify an event tiramisu, but the final decoration must be checked on the real dessert inside its selected box. Approve the lettering, handling and closed-lid clearance with a filled sample; neither the stencil material nor a photograph establishes display life.

For event caterers and dessert brands pairing cocoa decoration with a tiramisu container.

1. Select a suitable stencil and approved design

Begin with the finished dessert and its container. Measure the flat area available on the cocoa surface and check how the design will look once the matching lid is closed. Acrylic and flexible film are different stencil options; compare their thickness, handling and cleanability for your own process. Ask for the actual stencil material and any relevant food-contact or sanitation guidance rather than assuming a generic “food grade” rating.

For letters with enclosed centres such as O, A or B, the artwork may need small connecting bridges to keep the inner shapes attached. Confirm the visible result with a prototype; bridge width depends on the design and cutting method, and this guide does not specify a universal production tolerance.

2. Trial the cocoa decoration

Chill and finish the tiramisu according to your validated food-handling procedure. Keep the stencil steady above the surface without touching the cream where that would damage presentation. Apply a small, even amount of cocoa, then lift the stencil carefully. Trial the exact cocoa, stencil, artwork, surface and timing you plan to use. Condensation and moisture can change the appearance, so inspect samples under your actual display conditions.

Do not treat a concept photograph as proof of sharpness after a specified number of hours or servings. Record the result immediately and after your intended display period; make adjustments based on that trial.

3. Approve the decoration with the container and lid

Measure the tallest finished decoration and topping. Close the matching lid on a representative filled container and check clearance, side contact and presentation. Catalogue capacity and external height do not reveal the space under the closed lid. Use the container size guide for the measurement method and sample-testing checklist for normal handling.

In the purchasing brief, record the artwork revision, stencil material and size, quantity, selected body and lid references, approved sample photo, destination and intended handling. If the dessert height, lid or artwork changes, repeat the affected fit check before placing a repeat order.
