Custom Services

Five customization options

Branding an existing box: Clear Dessert Pack lists logo stickers, paper sleeves, paper bags, ribbons and acrylic cocoa stencils. Select the box and lid before developing matching accessories. These options describe branding and presentation; they do not by themselves confirm direct printing on the plastic, a new box mould or a custom container size.

For a structural change or direct-print request, submit the drawing, material, quantity and intended use for a separate feasibility review. Confirm tooling, MOQ, sample cost and timing in the written quotation. Prepare your branding quotation brief before approving artwork.
