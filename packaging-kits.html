Buying information & FAQs +
Can we mix different box sizes and accessories in one packaging kit order?

Send the box models, quantity per size and accessory list for review. Mixed configurations depend on availability and the minimums for each component. Confirm fit, artwork and the component packing list before ordering.

What is the MOQ for custom packaging kits?

MOQ depends on the box model, quantity per component, printing and customisation. Request a quotation for your proposed kit; a single universal minimum does not apply to every combination.

How are packaging kit components packed for international transit?

Packing depends on the component and selected model. Confirm whether boxes are nested or individually protected, how lids are packed, and whether sleeves and bags are supplied flat. Request carton dimensions, gross weights and the agreed protection method before comparing freight.

What should I send for a complete dessert packaging quotation?

Provide the dessert size, box and lid selection, quantity by model, sleeve or sticker artwork, carrying-bag requirements, intended handling conditions and delivery destination. Review a complete sample combination before approving the final specification.
