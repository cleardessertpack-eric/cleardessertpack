Glass vs Plastic Tiramisu Containers: Choosing for Bakery Sales

By Lucy

Material selectionPublished November 11, 2026

Choose the service model before the material Choose a tiramisu container around how you sell, serve and recover the packaging. Compare a glass dish for table service with a plastic box for takeaway only after defining the complete pack, including any cover and outer protection. A material name alone does not confirm lid fit, leak resistance or suitability for your process.

## 1. Define what happens after filling Write down whether the dessert stays on the premises, leaves with the customer or moves between kitchens. A reusable service model also needs an approved collection, cleaning and inspection process. Do not assume that a glass dish or a plastic box is suitable for repeated use simply because it survives one trial.

For bakeries, dessert producers and packaging buyers selecting empty tiramisu containers.

For takeaway, record who carries the pack and whether it remains upright. For table service, check how staff support the filled dish and how customers access the dessert.

2. Compare real formats, not equal capacity labels Our catalogue includes PS and PET plastic containers and five oval glass dishes. The OGBD-08L glass dish is listed at 0.8 L, 20.5 × 13.7 × 4.8 cm and 0.42 kg. The E1300 PET container is listed at 1300 ml and 18 × 13 × 6.4 cm. These are different formats, not controlled samples proving one material is better.

Use outside dimensions to check shelves and outer packs. Measure internal space and test the finished dessert separately. Capacity does not determine dessert weight or servings.

3. Confirm the cover and filled weight Ask which lid or cover is included and identify its material separately from the body. The glass dish range requires separate cover confirmation; a matching lid must not be assumed from capacity.

Compare the mass of the dessert, container, cover and outer pack together. For transport, also check protection between pieces and the quoted carton specification. Empty dish weight is not shipping weight.

4. Approve the selected use Test the shortlisted complete packs through the intended filling, storage, handling and serving process. Record damage, lid interference and ease of access. Obtain the exact model instructions before approving heating, freezing, cleaning or reuse. Choose the option that meets the agreed requirements, then retain its sample and component list for repeat orders.
